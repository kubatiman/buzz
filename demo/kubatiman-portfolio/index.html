About

Kubatiman is a composer and producer working at the edge of cinematic score and dark ambient-electro.

Every record starts the same way — not with a sound, but with a feeling that hasn't found language yet. Grief, distance, the particular quiet of an empty room. From there the studio becomes a translation booth: modular synthesis, processed strings, and field recordings layered until the feeling has a shape you can listen to.

That approach has carried Kubatiman's music into short films, gallery installations, and late-night playlists in equal measure — music built to score the parts of life nobody scores.
